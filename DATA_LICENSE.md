## 1. Original Simulation and Benchmark Datasets — CC BY 4.0

All 26 standalone data files under `03_paper_reproduction/` and the inline reference spectrum embedded in `03_paper_reproduction/fig5_fwbic_3d_sweep/Fig6re.m` were generated by the authors for *"Wavevector-Decoupled Tensor Architecture for High-Throughput Nanophotonic Simulations"* and are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))**:

- **Figure 2 (`03_paper_reproduction/fig2_euv_multilayer/outputs/`, 13 files):**
  - `data.json`, `data_all.json`, `pdps_cdf_32D_ctrl.csv`, `pdps_cdf_32D_top.csv` — Sobol screening statistics and conditional PDPS cumulative distributions produced by the PyTorch Tensor SMM survey.
  - `optimize_seed42.json`, `optimize_seed123.json`, `optimize_seed456.json`, `optimize_seed789.json`, `optimize_seed7.json`, `optimize_seed2024.json` — Six-seed Adam gradient-refinement summaries.
  - `ad_fd_result.json`, `ad_fd_scaling.json`, `verification_report.json` — 80-layer reverse-mode automatic differentiation vs. central finite-difference verification summaries.
- **Figure 3 (`03_paper_reproduction/fig3_mldg_kspace_diag/reference/`, 4 files):**
  - `f065_re1000.mat`, `f065_relayer.mat`, `layerres.mat`, `layerwvl.mat` — Archived device diffraction-efficiency curves and bare-film channel transfer amplitudes generated by RCWA and Tensor SMM calculations.
- **Figure 4 and Table 1 — MLDG (`03_paper_reproduction/fig4_mldg_tolerance/`, 5 files):**
  - `inputs/mldg100.npz`, `inputs/mldg1000.npz` — Sampled two-layer thickness grids and nominal MLDG parameters for the paired FMMAX CPU benchmark.
  - `reference/Batched_Cascade2.mat`, `reference/RETICOLOres.mat` — Archived $100 \times 100$ target-order efficiency maps computed by LIVIA and native RETICOLO.
  - `reference/fmmax_cpu_timings.csv` — Archived workstation timing records for the 100-state and 1,000-state FMMAX MLDG benchmarks (Table 1 and SI Section S9).
- **Figure 5 and Table 1 — Dual Grating (`03_paper_reproduction/fig5_fwbic_3d_sweep/`, 4 standalone files + 1 inline array):**
  - `reference/figure5_slices.mat`, `reference/wavexisao_nopar.mat` — Five cross-sections of the 3D LIVIA transmission volume and the 301-point native RETICOLO reference spectrum.
  - `reference/fmmax_shift_scaling.csv` — Archived workstation timing and full-block accuracy records for the dual-grating 1-to-1,001 lateral-displacement FMMAX benchmark (Table 1, $187.95\times$ at 1,001 offsets, and SI Section S9).
  - `reference/XYZ.mat` and the 301-point `COMSOL_full` array in `Fig6re.m` (line 41) — Finite-element electric-field samples (`[x, y, |E|]` at $\lambda = 1.610\,\mu\mathrm{m}$) and zero-order TE transmission spectrum exported from the authors' independent COMSOL Multiphysics verification models.

## 2. Tabulated Optical Constants — Public Domain

1. **`01_core_operators/matlab_reticolo/get_Si_nk.m`** (216 rows, $0.4000\text{--}1.3053\,\mu\mathrm{m}$):
   - Entry `main/Si/Vuye-20C` from [RefractiveIndex.INFO](https://refractiveindex.info/) ([`polyanskiy/refractiveindex.info-database`](https://github.com/polyanskiy/refractiveindex.info-database), [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).
   - Primary source: G. Vuye, S. Fisson, V. Nguyen Van, Y. Wang, J. Rivory, and F. Abelès, *Thin Solid Films* **233**, 166–170 (1993).
2. **`01_core_operators/matlab_reticolo/get_SiO2_nk.m`** (102 rows, $0.21\text{--}6.70\,\mu\mathrm{m}$):
   - Entry `main/SiO2/Malitson` from [RefractiveIndex.INFO](https://refractiveindex.info/) ([CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).
   - Primary source: I. H. Malitson, *J. Opt. Soc. Am.* **55**, 1205–1209 (1965).
3. **EUV constants at $\lambda = 13.5\,\mathrm{nm}$ (`03_paper_reproduction/fig2_euv_multilayer/common_ablation.py`):**
   - Standard Mo and Si complex refractive indices from the CXRO X-Ray Interactions With Matter database (B. L. Henke, E. M. Gullikson, and J. C. Davis, *At. Data Nucl. Data Tables* **54**, 181–342, 1993).
